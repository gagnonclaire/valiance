# Book Two - Empress

The daughter lies cradled in silky, crimson folds. Light from a high window illuminates webs of veins under her translucent skin. She sleeps soundly, sheltered from the cutting winds howling outside.

The empress is away, her child left alone and unaware in her absence. The empress has been away for a very long time.

-

Files lay haphazardly about the archives. Several rows deep, a lone researcher scanned through envelope after envelope. Her darkening eyes had grown unattentive, and she continued unaware of a quiet observer.

She continued for some time here, moving from envelope to envelope. Matching strange symbols, cross referencing case numbers. There was something to it, just at the edge of her reach. Her spectator spoke, freezing her.

"Isn't it a bit late, doctor?"

She took a moment to catch her breath, to find her voice.

"Ennin- how long have you been there?"
"You need to go home. You're going to get yourself hurt."
"I'm fine, I just have some things I need to piece together."

Agent Ennin made a show of scanning the racks of raided documents.

"Just a few things?"
"Ok, yeah, I'll clean it up when I'm done."
"Or right now."

The two traded gazes. Ennin crossed her arms, raising her head. The doctor slouched in response, resigning to exhaustion. With a sigh, she began to sort her mess of papers back into their cases.

"Stay safe, Dr. Vega"
"It's not my first late night, I can handle myself."
"That's not what I meant."

-

She flinched when she felt the cold breath on her neck. They were only two now, keeper and daughter, all other allegiances broken. The keeper felt its way down to the daughters waist, innumerable nails tracing with intent.

"You've been through so much, child. The corpses waded through, the blood and oil spilled."

The daughter reached a hand to her own shoulder, cowering into its chilled flesh. A sharp breath, followed by a choking quiet. The keeper continued, bringing another free hand to rest upon the same shoulder. Hand after hand followed, forming a cocoon of bony, umbral silk.

"Taken from your home. abandoned by your Empress. The world holds no compassion for you."

Steady talons crept from beneath the cloth, finding their way through the gaps until nails found purchase in the daughter's waist. Slowly, excruciatingly, they pierced the skin. For a moment, she resisted. She could fight. Paint the wall of this cabin with old, tainted blood. She knew she could turn this cabin into a slaughter house, if she wanted. Both of them knew.

"Strung along by death, never truly allowed it's embrace. But I'm here now, to give you what you've needed so badly."

Tension slipped, relaxing into a paralysis. The keepeer's nails dug deeper. Blackened blood began to drip. A choked whine, caught somewhere between pleasure and agony, broke her silence. It came slowly and softly, as the flow turned crimson.

"A release."

-

"hold on to this"

She held a small device out towards her companion. A simple detonator.

"What? Why would -"

With her other arm throwing the second bag over her already burdened shoulder, she cut in,

"keep it close, just in case"

Too stunned to protest, Calliope tucked the strange offering into her armored vest.

-

The sputtering of a dying flare did a poor job of masking the struggle. In it's last flashes, a woman pulled a massive blade from the split skull of a shock trooper. A response team of this size, this far into the subcity, was unheard of. Two shots rang out from below as the flare let loose its last flickers of light.

Further and further inward, she followed the clamor. Stepping carefully over corpses, driven by primal force brought to heel by decades of experience. A fork in the road, with two more dead shock troopers standing sentinel. One of Ceta's bags, abandoned and split open to reveal several small, wrapped packages. Another deafening shot, a caliber far beyond what such close quarters would demand. Ears still ringing, she followed the shot left.

More bodies, dozens now, of the best IDCon had to offer. And another of Ceta's bags along with some strange shards that seemed to shimmer in defiance of the darkness. She trudged through the rivers of blood towards their source. Were it not for the clash of metal on metal, she would have assumed it flowed from the corpse her partner. Most of it probably still did, she thought, as she stepped over a massive, sundered rifle and crossed the final threshold.

-

"I need your detonator"

Ceta stood over the headless body of the response team captain, her right arm reaching backwards towards Calliope.

"mine's busted"

Broken from her stupor, the agent reached shakily into her vest. It was clear that much of the blood, now pooling at their feet, was Ceta's.

"Are you ok?"

No response came. Ceta snatched the detonator and, with a dramatic pause, closed the trigger.

-

"you smell nice"

Calliope paused, stunned. She sat up straight, laugh cut short by a sharp pain.

"I smell like blood and sweat"
"yeah, the blood"
"Excuse me?"
"the smell, it's kind of airy"
"what?"
"airy, like it's light, and sort of flor-
"i know what airy is, i- it's just blood, ceta."

Ceta pulled away, dropping her gaze. the two sat for a quiet moment. calliope moved to rest a hand on her shoulder, and ceta spoke softly

"just blood"

The low trickle of water over old stonework filled their silence, punctuated by deep groans of the megastructure above. Calliope leaned in with an exaggerated sigh, coaxing half a smile.

"mine just smells kinda musty, like mold or something, it's been worse since-"

Cali pulled her closer, words cut short as lips met.

-

The two paused to catch their breath.

"I can smell it now."

Ceta sat up, a single eyebrow raised in Cali's direction

"- your blood, I mean."

Ceta leaned away, easing into a meandering laugh.

"oh yeah?"
"Yeah. It reminds me of the smell after it rains"
"petrichor"
"What?"
"that's what that's called"

IDCON agents are not necessarily known for their expressive demeanors, but in that moment Cali let slip a short giggle. Ceta's face beamed, a strange visage of bloodstained mirth. Her laugh erased into a soft whine as she leaned back inwards.

"Cali, I-"

The room fell breathless, cut by anticipation. With the rest of her words swallowed, Ceta ceded to gravity and the two of them fell into eachother.

"I'm really happy you're here"

-

From their crumbling perch, they watched the city burn. Sanguine flames twisted up the skyline, weaving their tapestry towards the heavens. Answering from above, a piercing crimson rain tore through the upper levels of the city. At the highest point, atop the Alburn Monolith, the mark of the Empress flashed through sheets of blood and flame.

The scaffold began to give, but the Daughter stood immovable. The only shelter from this storm. Elizabeth struggled to find solid footing, reaching out with one hand while the other shielded her face from the lacerating downpour.

"Ceta, please, we have to go"

The Daughter drew her razor.

"Ceta, you can't, there's no way"

Steel buckled, bringing low the structure. Elizabeth found hold around the Daughter's waist as the building collapsed around them.

"We could go underground, maybe-"

"Then go."

"What? I don't know the subcity, I don't know where to go. And there's nothing you can do here, no way you can..."

The Daughter pulled Elizabeth upright. Impossibly, the floor beneath them held sturdy. The rain seemed to bend around the two, and the flames grow meek around them.

"I have to"

Finding her composure, Elizabeth pushed herself away. She managed to hold herself upright, even if only just. She spoke between shaking breaths.

"Ceta. if you go, I can't follow. I won't go with you."

"You never do, Liz."

One sure step brought the Daughter to the edge. She stopped for a moment, and time with her. A second step here could not be taken back, and the weight hung over the two of them like the end of the world. Without a work, Ceta took the second step. In an instant, the sanctuary broke. Elizabeth was left to brave the edge of ruin, and the Daughter plunged into its heart.

-

The world hemorrhaged below, reality itself churning in on itself in waves of red. The Empress, hands held outward, struggled to regain control of her apocalypse.

Thunder crashed as her porcelain skin cracked. It fell to the ground in bloody shards, revealing a heart beating to the rhythm of the storm. Crystalline razors coalesced into three sets of sanguine wings. She rose from the surface of the imperial spire, held aloft by a bone-shattering pressure.

The Daughter grasped at the edge of consciousness, pulling herself up against the impossible weight of the end. A single jagged shard congealed in her hand, all she could manage. She fixed her fading sight on the six-winged silhouette of the Empress. The distance seemed impossible. Step by excruciating step, she pulled herself closer. Before she could find her reach, the Empress turned, just enough to catch her desperate eyes.

Now upright, the daughter made a desperate lunge. Her flesh parted as she passed through the bladed wings, but her free hand found purchase on the shoulder of the Empress. In this final motion, the two found themselves face to face, the dagger's point kissing the surface of the Empress' exposed heart.

"Daughter..."

The point found little resistance. There was no fight left to be had, only a final decision to be made. The Empress moved her hand over the Daughter's, making no effort to change the blade's course.

"You will never belong in their world."

The blade's tip passed the event horizon softly, and the surface of the heart pulled apart without a sound.

"I know"

All at once, the world began to move once again. The force of the collision sent the two into the freefall off of the edge of the spire. They fell, trailed by a screaming geyser. The final cry of the dying blood.

"I loved you, Daughter..."

The storm quieted as the pair fell further. They between them hardly held enough breath for a single word, and the earth fast approached.

"I know"

-

She splashed through a world inverted, watching her own body fall in cascades. She sank slowly, the sight of the surface fading softly. She saw her hand move before she could feel it. Something imperceptible pulled at it, an invisible thread still attached to the other side.

She reached towards it, driven by some solemn curiosity. Before she had even commanded it, her hand grasped another, a mirror image of its own. It was soft, the only thing to be felt in this void. She devoted her entire self - if she even still had one - to this hold. As the last lights of the world above faded, a strange sensation hit her. The subtle smell of honey, cutting the agony as the world dimmed.
